# Aniyomi overview

Aniyomi is an Android app for watching anime and reading manga, built on top of Mihon (the successor
to Tachiyomi). It is a **client**: it ships with no content. Extensions add sources, the same way
add-ons add sources to Stremio.

- Website: [aniyomi.org](https://aniyomi.org/)
- Source: [aniyomiorg/aniyomi](https://github.com/aniyomiorg/aniyomi) (Apache-2.0)
- Platform: Android phones, tablets and Android TV

## What it does well

- **Library management** — categories, tracking, "continue watching", bulk actions.
- **Extensions** — hundreds of community sources, installed from a repository inside the app.
- **Tracking** — MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, Shikimori and others.
- **Downloads** — save episodes locally, with quality selection.
- **Player** — built on mpv, with gestures, subtitles, audio tracks and speed control.
- **Backups** — full library and settings export, which is what makes switching forks painless.

## What it does not do

- It does not provide or host content.
- It is not a Stremio add-on; the two ecosystems are separate.
- The original project moves slowly, which is why [forks](/guides/anime/forks) exist.

## Legal note

Extensions point at third-party sites. Whether watching from them is legal depends on your country
and on the source. This site does not host or link to copyrighted media.
